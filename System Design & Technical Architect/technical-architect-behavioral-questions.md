# Technical Architect Behavioral Questions

Architect interviews include behavioral questions because architects influence teams, resolve ambiguity, and make decisions with long-term consequences.

## Common Questions

**Q: Tell me about a time you made a major architecture decision.**  
A: Explain context, options considered, decision criteria, stakeholders, implementation, result, and what you learned.

**Q: Tell me about a time you disagreed with another senior engineer.**  
A: Focus on data, tradeoffs, respectful discussion, and alignment. Avoid ego-driven language.

**Q: How do you influence teams without direct authority?**  
A: Build trust, explain reasoning, document decisions, listen to concerns, provide examples, and support teams during implementation.

**Q: How do you handle technical debt?**  
A: Make debt visible, connect it to delivery or risk, prioritize incrementally, and avoid stopping all feature work unless risk is severe.

**Q: How do you mentor architects or senior developers?**  
A: Review designs, ask guiding questions, discuss tradeoffs, encourage documentation, and help them think in systems.

## Strong Architect Traits

- Clarity
- Ownership
- Tradeoff thinking
- Communication
- Humility
- Technical depth
- Business alignment
- Risk awareness
