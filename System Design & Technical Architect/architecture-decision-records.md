# Architecture Decision Records

Architecture Decision Records, or ADRs, document important technical decisions, the context behind them, alternatives considered, and consequences. They help teams remember why a decision was made.

## ADR Structure

- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives considered
- Consequences
- Date
- Owners

## Interview Q&A

**Q: Why use ADRs?**  
A: They reduce repeated debates, preserve technical reasoning, help onboarding, and make tradeoffs explicit.

**Q: What decisions deserve an ADR?**  
A: Database choice, architecture style, cloud provider, messaging technology, authentication approach, deployment strategy, and major tradeoffs.

**Q: How detailed should an ADR be?**  
A: Short but complete. It should capture the reason and consequence, not become a long design document.
