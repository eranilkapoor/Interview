## Interview Q&A

**Q: How do you handle a prompt you have never seen before?**  
A: Use the standard framework. Clarify requirements, identify entities and flows, make simple assumptions, design the core path, then improve for scale and reliability.

**Q: Should you draw a diagram?**  
A: Yes, even verbally. A clear component diagram helps the interviewer follow your thinking.
