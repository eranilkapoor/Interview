# Salary, Notice Period, and Offer Negotiation

This round tests professionalism and clarity. Be honest, confident, and specific. Do not overexplain.

## Salary Questions

**Q: What is your expected salary?**  
A: "Based on my experience, current market range, and the responsibilities of this role, I am expecting around X to Y. I am open to discussing the complete compensation structure."

**Q: Is salary negotiable?**  
A: "Yes, I am open to a fair discussion based on role scope, benefits, growth opportunity, and overall compensation."

**Q: Why are you expecting this hike?**  
A: "The expectation is based on my experience level, technical ownership, delivery responsibility, and current market range for similar roles."

## Notice Period Questions

**Q: What is your notice period?**  
A: State the official notice period clearly. If buyout or early release is possible, mention it without promising what you cannot control.

**Q: Can you join immediately?**  
A: If not, say: "I will need to follow my current notice period, but I can check early release or buyout possibilities after offer discussion."

## Offer Questions

**Q: Do you have other offers?**  
A: Be truthful. You can say: "I am in discussion with a few companies, but I am evaluating based on role fit, growth, technology, and compensation."

**Q: What matters most in your next role?**  
A: Mention role quality, learning, technical ownership, culture, stability, and fair compensation.

## Common Pitfalls

- Giving inconsistent salary numbers.
- Making false claims about offers.
- Sounding only money-driven.
- Promising impossible joining dates.
- Negotiating aggressively before role fit is clear.
