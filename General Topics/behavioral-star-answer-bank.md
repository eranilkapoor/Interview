# Behavioral STAR Answer Bank

STAR means Situation, Task, Action, Result. For senior interviews, add one more part: Learning. This shows maturity and continuous improvement.

## Story Template

**Situation:** What was happening?  
**Task:** What were you responsible for?  
**Action:** What did you do?  
**Result:** What changed because of your action?  
**Learning:** What did you improve afterward?

## Stories to Prepare

### 1. Production Incident

- Situation: Application outage, performance issue, deployment failure, or data issue.
- Action: Stabilized system, communicated impact, found root cause, deployed fix.
- Result: Service restored, prevention added.
- Learning: Added monitoring, tests, rollback plan, or runbook.

### 2. Architecture Decision

- Situation: Existing system had scaling, maintainability, or performance limits.
- Action: Compared options, proposed design, aligned stakeholders.
- Result: Improved reliability, speed, cost, or developer productivity.

### 3. Conflict Resolution

- Situation: Disagreement between developers, QA, product, or client.
- Action: Listened, clarified facts, aligned on priority, proposed compromise.
- Result: Decision made and delivery continued.

### 4. Mentoring

- Situation: Junior developer needed support.
- Action: Pairing, reviews, explanation, task breakdown.
- Result: Developer became more independent.

### 5. Delivery Under Pressure

- Situation: Tight timeline or critical release.
- Action: Prioritized scope, tracked risks, communicated daily, protected quality.
- Result: Release completed with controlled risk.

### 6. Technical Debt

- Situation: Codebase slowed delivery or caused defects.
- Action: Identified debt, proposed incremental refactor, added tests.
- Result: Reduced bugs or improved delivery speed.

## Behavioral Questions

- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Tell me about a difficult stakeholder.
- Tell me about a time you led without authority.
- Tell me about a time you handled ambiguity.
- Tell me about a time you improved a process.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with an architect or manager.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly.
