## How to Use This Folder

Prepare 8-10 strong stories from your real experience, then reuse them across different questions. For each story, keep the structure clear: situation, responsibility, action, result, and learning. Senior interviews reward clarity, ownership, maturity, and tradeoff thinking more than memorized answers.
