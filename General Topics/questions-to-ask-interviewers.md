# Questions to Ask Interviewers

Asking good questions shows maturity. For senior roles, ask about architecture, team ownership, delivery process, quality expectations, cloud maturity, and business impact.

## Questions for HR

- What are the next steps in the interview process?
- What is the work model: remote, hybrid, or office?
- What is the expected joining timeline?
- How is performance evaluated?
- What growth opportunities are available for this role?

## Questions for Hiring Manager

- What are the main responsibilities in the first 90 days?
- What are the biggest challenges the team is facing?
- How is work planned and prioritized?
- What is the team structure?
- How do engineering, product, QA, and DevOps collaborate?

## Questions for Technical Architect Panel

- What are the main architectural goals this year?
- Are there active migrations or modernization efforts?
- What are the main scalability or reliability challenges?
- How are architecture decisions documented?
- How does the team handle technical debt?

## Questions for Delivery Lead Panel

- What delivery methodology does the team follow?
- How are estimates, risks, and dependencies tracked?
- What are common causes of delivery delay here?
- How are production incidents handled?
- How is release readiness measured?

## Questions for Cloud Expert Panel

- Which cloud provider and services are most used?
- How mature is the Infrastructure as Code setup?
- What monitoring and alerting tools are used?
- How are cloud costs reviewed?
- What are the disaster recovery expectations?

## Questions to Avoid

- Questions already answered clearly in the job description.
- Salary-only questions in technical rounds.
- Negative questions about company stability unless framed professionally.
- Questions that suggest you are not interested in ownership.
