# MR and Managerial Round Questions

MR rounds test whether you can operate in a team, own delivery, manage ambiguity, communicate with stakeholders, handle conflict, mentor people, and make practical decisions under constraints. For senior roles, this round often decides whether the interviewer trusts you with responsibility.

## Common Questions and Answer Direction

**Q: How do you manage competing priorities?**  
A: Clarify business impact, urgency, dependencies, and risk. Align with stakeholders, break work into milestones, and communicate tradeoffs early.

**Q: How do you handle missed deadlines?**  
A: Identify root cause, communicate early, re-plan scope or timeline, protect quality, and prevent recurrence through better estimation or risk tracking.

**Q: How do you mentor junior developers?**  
A: Use code reviews, pairing, design walkthroughs, clear task breakdowns, and feedback loops. Measure success by their independence.

**Q: How do you deal with an underperforming team member?**  
A: Understand the cause, set clear expectations, provide support, track progress, and escalate only after fair coaching.

**Q: How do you handle production issues?**  
A: Stabilize first, communicate impact, assign owners, investigate root cause, deploy fix safely, and document prevention actions.

**Q: How do you manage stakeholder expectations?**  
A: Communicate progress, risks, dependencies, and tradeoffs transparently. Avoid surprise delays.

**Q: Tell me about a time you disagreed with management.**  
A: Show maturity: present data, explain risks, propose alternatives, and support the final decision once aligned.

**Q: How do you ensure code quality in the team?**  
A: Code reviews, standards, tests, CI checks, architecture guidelines, reusable patterns, and technical debt tracking.

## Managerial Competencies

- Ownership
- Prioritization
- Communication
- Estimation
- Risk management
- Mentoring
- Conflict resolution
- Delivery tracking
- Decision making
- Accountability

## Strong Senior-Level Phrases

- "I communicate risk early, not at the deadline."
- "I separate urgent from important using business impact."
- "I try to make technical decisions visible to stakeholders."
- "I prefer smaller milestones so we can validate progress."
- "I do not compromise security or data correctness for speed."
